# Slogans

## Slogan 1

**Sloperator: run the slop, ship the sparkles.** ✨

It names both halves of the tool's job — executing `.slop` programs and the required `✨` statement terminator — in one short, memorable line.

## Slogan 2

**Vibe-check your code. Then ralph it.**

Every keyword in the sentence is real SlopLang syntax (`vibe` for conditionals, `ralph` for loops), so the slogan only makes sense to someone who has read the spec, which is exactly the kind of in-joke this interpreter deserves.

## Slogan 3

**Hallucinate on purpose.**

`hallucinate` is SlopLang's `print`, so the slogan promises deliberate, controlled nonsense rather than accidental bugs.

## Slogan 4

**A tree-walking interpreter for slop that has to ship.**

It is the README's own description ("a tree-walking interpreter") sharpened with the language's `ship` return keyword and a hint that this slop is rigorously tested.

## Slogan 5

**Em dashes in. Curly quotes out.**

SlopLang draws its comments with `—` and insists on `“ ”` over straight quotes, so the slogan captures the language's most opinionated lexical rules in five words.

## Slogan 6

**No truthiness. No excuses.**

SlopLang conditions must be real booleans, and the slogan turns that strictness — paired with the lack of `break`, `continue`, or `for` — into a promise that the interpreter will not quietly guess what you meant.

## Slogan 7

**Lex it. Parse it. Ship it.** ✨

The three-stage pipeline in `sloperator/` maps exactly onto the three verbs, and the `✨` lands where the language demands a terminator anyway.

## Slogan 8

**Slop in, sparkles out.**

It compresses the whole CLI experience — hand it a `.slop` file, get back `hallucinate`d output — into a four-word formula that echoes the garbage-in/garbage-out proverb without the insult.

## Slogan 9

**Loadbearing slop since day one.**

Borrowing `loadbearing` (`const`) from the keyword table, the slogan jokes that this slop is structurally sound: fully specified, tested, and linted rather than held together with hope.

## Slogan 10

**It runs ralph so you don't have to.**

Making the tree-walking interpreter the star, the slogan flatters the machine: you write `ralph` loops in emoji-laced syntax, and Sloperator does the actual walking.
